# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

MD View (published on the Microsoft Store as **MD Viewer PWA**) is a Markdown viewer and editor that runs entirely on your device. This policy covers the website at https://md-viewer.t21.dev and the Microsoft Store app, which loads that same website.

## What we collect

Nothing. MD View has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Your files

Files you open, paste or create are read and rendered in your browser or in the app on your device. They are never uploaded. PDF and text export also happen on your device.

## What is stored on your device

To restore your work between sessions, MD View saves the following in your browser's local storage and IndexedDB on your device:

- the text of your open tabs
- a list of recently opened files (names and dates) and, where your browser supports it, file handles so you can reopen them
- your settings, such as theme, accent colour and code highlighting theme

None of this is sent anywhere. You can clear it at any time by closing your tabs or by clearing site data for md-viewer.t21.dev (or uninstalling the app).

## Network requests

- **Loading the app.** The app's code is downloaded from md-viewer.t21.dev, hosted on Cloudflare. As with any website, Cloudflare processes standard request data (such as IP address and user agent) to deliver and protect the site. We do not use this data to identify or track you. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
- **Images in your documents.** If a Markdown file links to an image on another website, that image is loaded from that website, which may see your IP address. Documents without remote images make no such requests.

After the first visit, the app works offline.

## Children

MD View does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.
